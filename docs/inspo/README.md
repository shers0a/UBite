# Inspiration

Drop reference images here. No structure required — just dump files in and I will read them.

## What is most useful to collect

**UB official material** — the most valuable thing in this folder if it exists.
Brand manual, logo files, the official colour palette, screenshots of existing UB digital
products (the admissions app, the student portal, `unibuc.ro`). We decided UBite has its own
identity, but it has to survive being shown to the University, so knowing the house style
matters even when we deviate from it.

**Apps you actually like** — screenshots, not links. What matters is the specific screen that
made you stop, not the product as a whole.

**The signature moment** — anything that shows a single piece of status information well. A
live occupancy display, a wait-time estimate, a transit arrival board, a queue indicator. The
crowding indicator is the component this whole system will be recognised by, and it is the one
worth over-researching.

**Typography and colour references** — type specimens, palettes, anything with a mood you want.

**Anti-references** — screenshots of things you specifically do *not* want. These are as useful
as the positive ones and much rarer, because nobody collects them.

**The canteen itself** — photos from the site visit. The room, the queue, the wall where the
kiosk would go, the lighting. The design has to work in that actual space, on a tablet read
from three metres, in whatever light is there.

## Naming

Only if it is no effort: a short prefix helps me sort them quickly.

```
ub-        official UB material
like-      something you want to borrow from
avoid-     something you want to steer away from
canteen-   photos of the actual place
type-      typography references
color-     palette references
```

Anything unnamed is fine too. Tell me what you see in it and I will work from that.
